t06 — The receive audio path, proven with a plain tone

Question: does sound played into the virtual device actually
reach the application? A 1500 Hz sine wave — the same offset
the main window was set to — was generated by the lab (14 s,
48 kHz, 16-bit, half scale) and played into the null sink,
whose monitor is the application's input (t03).

$ paplay --device=lab tone-1500hz-48k.wav

While it played, the main window's input level meter read
81 dB, full green (screenshot js8call-waterfall-tone.png in
this lab folder). With the sink silent the meter sits at the
bottom. Sound in a computer is just numbers changing hands;
here each hand was watched: file, player, sink, monitor,
application, meter.

And then the important negative: a steady carrier is not a
JS8 signal, and the decoder treated it as such. After the
playback:

$ cat ~/.local/share/JS8Call/ALL.TXT
2026-10-30 14:06:17  14.078 MHz  JS8
2026-10-30 14:09:14  14.078 MHz  JS8

Those two lines are session headers from the two starts.
Nothing was decoded, nothing was logged — exactly what an
honest receiver should do with a laboratory tone.
